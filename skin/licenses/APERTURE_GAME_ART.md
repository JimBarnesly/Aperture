# Aperture game-system artwork

Copyright 2026 Aperture contributors. SPDX-License-Identifier: GPL-3.0-or-later.

The system and utility illustrations in `media/aperture/games/systems/` are
original vector artwork made for Aperture. Editable SVG sources are in the
repository at `skin/artwork/games/`, generated by `tools/build_game_art.py`.
The PNG versions are rendered from those sources and carry the same license.

Hardware shapes identify the represented systems. Product and company names
belong to their respective owners. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.
No commercial game artwork, ROM, product photograph, or third-party theme asset
is included in this set. The project GPL text is available in the repository.
